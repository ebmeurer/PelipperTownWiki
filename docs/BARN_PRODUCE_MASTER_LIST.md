# Barn produce master list

Barn products for all 731 built-in Pokémon. Species products take priority over
the first listed type. Deerling and Sawsbuck use the current season instead.

A fed Barn resident produces one item overnight, even with Breeding turned off.
Residents eat nearby pasture grass when they can graze, or hay from the Barn trough.
Hay needed by ordinary livestock is reserved first. Collect goods from the floor.
If there is no safe space, the product stays protected until it can be placed or recovered.

Petting that day, manually or with an Auto-Petter, gives quality-capable goods silver quality,
gold at 150 bond, or iridium at 230 bond. Without petting, they remain regular quality.
Materials and artisan goods stay regular quality. Pokémon Eggs are separate from these goods.

The same products are used for PokéRanch morning finds. Ranch finds use the owner's
Breeding skill odds and bonuses rather than the Barn's feeding requirement.
Persistent Mega and legendary forms use their underlying species' product.
