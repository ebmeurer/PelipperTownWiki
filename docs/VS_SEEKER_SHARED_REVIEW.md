# Shared-owner team review

Scope: every NPC owning any species that appeared on at least eight NPC rosters before this review (that NPC plus seven others). Each owner counts once across all four tiers, including earlier evolutionary stages. This is a thematic review, not a new seven-owner cap. The limits remain three vanilla owners, ten overall, and three Pokémon sharing a type per team.

Reviewed **109 NPCs** and **30 qualifying species**. Applied five approved Fighting-type swaps and fifteen additional thematic substitutions. The changes are fixed authored choices, not random runtime assignments.
