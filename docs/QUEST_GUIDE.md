# Pelipper Town Complete Quest Guide

This guide explains how to unlock and complete every Pelipper Town journal
quest, one-time mail favor, friendship story, and recurring Pokémon request.
It is written as a companion to the
[Player Guide & Walkthrough](PLAYER_GUIDE.md). Use that guide for controls,
combat rules, party building, breeding, habitats, and the complete boss and
legendary reference.

This entire guide contains spoilers. Quest choices, encounter species, levels,
rewards, and late-game objectives are revealed below.

## Before starting a quest

Keep these rules in mind before accepting a difficult story or donating a
Pokémon:

1. **Read the Journal objective.** It updates when a quest changes stages. A
   quest that says to report to someone is not complete until that final
   conversation happens.
2. **Bring a healthy party.** Story opponents use ordinary health, status,
   moves, capture, and fainting rules. Group stories can place several enemies
   on the map at once.
3. **Carry several kinds of Balls.** Nearly every wild story opponent can be
   caught. Alex's Machop and Herdier are trainer-owned and cannot be caught.
4. **Make room before catching a required target.** A caught Pokémon goes to
   the party when there is room and otherwise goes to personal Pokémon Chest
   storage. The quest still advances either way.
5. **Do not donate a favorite by accident.** Pokémon given to a villager for a
   mail favor are permanently removed from the player's party or storage. Many
   appear afterward as permanent residents, but they are no longer player
   owned.
6. **Keep quest items until the final turn-in.** Summon items are consumed only
   after their encounter is created successfully. Willy can replace the first
   Smelly Bait or Harvey's Spare Suit if its quest is still active and the item
   is missing.
7. **Use the current Wild Pokémon page for targets.** Open **Partner Journal >
   Wild Pokémon** to find a required species. The page accounts for the current
   year, season, weather, time, research, settings, and installed content
   packs.

Leaving a story map or losing a fight usually does not fail its quest. Return
on another eligible day or during the listed time window. The Blue Foal and
Jimothy specifically require a catch, so defeating their targets delays the
next attempt instead of completing that stage.

## How Pelipper Town quests work

Pelipper Town uses four main quest types.

| Type | How it begins | Deadline | How it finishes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Friendship story | Reach the required hearts, then open a letter or enter the listed location | None | Finish the scene, battle, search, choice, and final conversation |
| One-time mail favor | Meet its hearts, caught-species, season, and progression requirements | None | Talk to the requester and donate the required Pokémon or item |
| Request-board job | Repair Robin's request board, then accept its Help Wanted posting | Stardew's normal Help Wanted deadline | Talk to the requester and hand in the exact target |
| Long journal quest | Read its introductory letter | None | Complete every tracked counter or named encounter |

### Mail delivery rules

Eligible one-time favors arrive in the mailbox. Willy's first favor has
priority, followed by Robin's request-board repair, then the remaining eligible
favors in their authored order.

- Willy's first letter can arrive from Spring 5 of Year 1 onward after the
  player chooses a starter and catches at least two species.
- Finishing a favor allows the next eligible letter to arrive the following
  morning.
- When an earlier favor remains open, new favor letters are spaced at least
  seven days apart.
- A player can have up to three unfinished one-time mail favors at once.
- A season-limited favor waits for one of its valid seasons. Missing the season
  does not lose the favor permanently.
- Opening the letter adds its quest to that player's Journal.

### Pokémon donations

For a Pokémon favor, speak to the requester while the quest is active. The
donation view opens the player's party and personal storage. Select one valid
Pokémon and confirm the permanent donation.

The game blocks an active partner, the player's last owned Pokémon, a mounted
or favorite Pokémon, a breeding parent, a fainted or ailing Pokémon, and a
Pokémon carrying items. Recall it, remove its favorite status, empty its cargo,
or choose another individual before trying again.

Every completed one-time favor gives 250 friendship with its requester. The
reward table below lists the additional reward. Ordinary request-board Pokémon
jobs give 150 friendship instead.

### Item turn-ins

Robin's 25 Wood repair uses the ordinary quest hand-in and can count Wood
anywhere in the backpack. For other item favors, place the complete requested
stack in the active toolbar slot, hold it, and speak to the requester. The
items are consumed only after confirmation.

## Recommended quest route

This route reduces waiting without forcing every quest into one year.

### Early game

1. Choose a starter, make two catches, and read Lewis's Battle League letter.
2. Reach one heart with Alex, Haley, Leah, Harvey, and Maru.
3. Complete **A Friendly Rival**, **A Very Important Favor**, and **A Face in
   the Glass**.
4. Complete Willy's **A New Deckhand**, then Robin's **A Place for Requests**.
5. Pick up the Moldy Cheese inside the Community Center and complete
   **Something Cheesy**.
6. Reach two hearts with Jas, Linus, Marnie, Pierre, Lewis, Abigail, Sebastian,
   Shane, Clint, and Jodi.
7. Complete the available two-heart stories and the first seasonal Battle
   League rungs.

### Mid game

1. Raise one owned Pokémon to level 25 for **Trouble in the Tide**.
2. Complete Harvey's **Clinic Companion**, then reach five hearts with Harvey
   for **The Window Watcher**.
3. Complete **Late Night Jams**, then raise Abigail and Sebastian to five hearts
   for **Ghost Club: Copycats**.
4. Build the Coop or Barn required by Marnie and Shane's donation favors.
5. Open the Railroad, meet the Wizard, and begin the higher-heart relic favors.
6. Continue catching distinct species. Several later favors require 15, 20,
   25, 30, or 40 caught species.

### Late game

1. Reach eight hearts with Shane and complete **The Flock Is Loose**.
2. Catch 40 species and meet the Wizard to begin **Four Signs in the Sky**.
3. Complete all three trainer rungs in all four seasons.
4. Catch 200 distinct species to finish the Battle League journal and earn the
   Champion's Crown.
5. Use the Crown at the Summit and at resolved legendary landmarks for the
   post-quest challenges described in the Player Guide.

## Robin's Pokémon homes

Robin sends these letters the morning after you reach each personal catch total.
Repeat wild catches count; gifts, trades, hatches, and evolutions do not. Each
player receives their own letters and quests, including existing saves already
past the milestones.

| Catches | Letter contents | Quest |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 15 | One PokéRanch Marker and its recipe | **A Place to Roam:** enclose a farm yard with at least four interior tiles using fences, buildings, or farm edges, place the marker inside, and assign one of your boxed Pokémon. |
| 20 | Pokémon Hut recipe | **A Home for a Helper:** craft a hut with **100 Wood and 100 Stone**, place it on the farm, and assign one of your boxed Pokémon. |

Opening the letter starts the quest. Its journal objective updates as you work.
The gifted marker is enough for the ranch quest; you don't need to craft a
second one. The hut quest requires at least one hut crafted by you. An assignment
belonging to another player does not complete either quest.

## Friendship and story quests

### A Friendly Rival

**Unlock:** Reach one heart, or 250 friendship points, with Alex and enter
Town.

**Preparation:** Bring one healthy partner around level 10 or higher. Type
coverage against Fighting and Normal opponents helps, but the battle can be
repeated until it is won.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Watch Alex's Town event and accept the challenge. Choosing to wait leaves
   the quest active.
2. If the battle did not begin in the event, speak to Alex later and challenge
   him through his quest dialogue.
3. Defeat his level 10 Machop and level 10 Herdier. These are trainer-owned
   Pokémon and cannot be caught.
4. Losing does not close the quest. Heal, speak to Alex, and try again.

**Reward:** 5 Great Balls.

### A Very Important Favor

**Unlock:** Reach one heart with Haley and read her letter.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Go to the Beach. Haley's currently configured partner is waiting on the
   open central shoreline.
2. Interact with the partner. It begins following the quest owner.
3. Speak to Haley. The partner does not need to be placed in the player's
   party or chest.
4. Read Haley's separate **The Strange Egg** letter when it arrives and claim
   the attached Egg.

**Reward:** 5 Ethers, 250 additional Haley friendship, and a Cosmog Egg through
the follow-up letter.

**Repeating Cosmog:** Once the favor is finished, challenge Haley with the VS
Seeker and win her tier 4, level 60 match. Every win hands over another Cosmog
Egg, so the family can be farmed and shiny-hunted on one save. The battle
journal's seven-day rematch lock is the limit; the challenge screen lists the
Egg alongside the usual balls, medicine, and gold.

Both the letter and rematch Cosmog Eggs have a 1-in-256 base shiny chance.
Training level 10, Shiny Charm, and Shiny Specialist each double the chance,
up to 1-in-32 with all three. Willy's Phione reward Eggs use the same odds.

### A Face in the Glass

**Unlock:** Reach one heart with Leah and read her letter.

**Preparation:** Bring Poké Balls if the disguised Pokémon should join the
party.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Enter Cindersap Forest after 7:00 PM.
2. Search immediately outside Leah's cottage. A disguised level 5 Zorua
   appears near the door.
3. Catch or defeat Zorua.
4. Speak to Leah after the Journal changes to the report stage.

**Reward:** 10 Poké Balls. A caught Zorua remains with the player.

### The Blue Foal

**Unlock:** Reach two hearts, or 500 friendship points, with Jas and enter
Cindersap Forest to see her event.

**Preparation:** Bring several strong Balls and a partner that can weaken a
level 15 target without defeating it.

**Walkthrough:**

1. After the event, search the western ranchlands of Cindersap Forest for a
   shiny level 15 Ponyta.
2. Catch Ponyta. Defeating it does not advance the quest. Leave the Forest and
   return for another attempt.
3. Speak to Jas while the captured Ponyta still belongs to the quest owner.
4. Choose one of the final outcomes. **Give the Ponyta to Jas** permanently
   removes it from the player's roster, places it in Marnie's fenced pasture,
   and awards 5 Revives. **Keep the Ponyta** leaves it in the player's roster
   and removes 250 Jas friendship. **Decide later** leaves the quest open.

The donation protections still apply to the Give choice. Recall Ponyta, remove
its favorite status, clear carried items, and make sure another owned Pokémon
remains if the donation is blocked.

### A Safe Place

**Unlock:** Reach two hearts with Linus and read his letter.

**Preparation:** Bring a healthy level 20 team and enough Balls for two
catchable opponents.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Enter the Backwoods path directly north of the farm between 3:00 PM and
   6:00 PM.
2. Find and speak to Linus on the open path.
3. The scene introduces a frightened level 20 Cubone, then a level 20 Pancham
   and level 20 Pangoro.
4. Catch or defeat Pancham and Pangoro. Linus joins the confrontation, and
   Cubone's story health cannot fall below 10 percent.
5. The victory scene completes the quest and leaves Cubone with Linus.

**Reward:** 1 Dark Honey and the Dark Honey recipe. Throw crafted Dark Honey on
safe land to call another level 20 Pancham and Pangoro encounter.

### The Long Way Home

**Unlock:** Reach two hearts with Marnie and read her letter.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Visit Marnie and speak to her at the ranch. This changes the Journal from
   the visit stage to the search stage.
2. Search Cindersap Forest near the Wizard's tower and interact with the
   missing level 20 Miltank.
3. Two level 20 Mightyena called the Howlers block the return. Both are wild
   story opponents and can be caught or defeated.
4. Choose either valid rescue route. Stay near Miltank and guide it east into
   the small fenced dirt pasture beside Marnie's ranch, or catch or defeat both
   Howlers to clear the danger immediately. Reaching only the ranch road or the
   larger grass field does not complete the escort route.
5. Let the reunion scene finish.

**Reward:** A permanent 10 percent discount on Marnie's applicable ranch shop
and animal prices.

### Jimothy's Fruit Run

**Unlock:** Reach two hearts with both Pierre and Lewis, then enter Town from
9:00 AM to 3:00 PM.

**Preparation:** Bring several accurate or high-tier Balls. Jimothy is
skittish, roams throughout Town, and must be caught.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Watch Pierre and Lewis chase the grey Mankey through Town.
2. Find Jimothy, a unique grey level 18 Mankey, and catch him. Defeating him
   makes him unavailable for the rest of that day. Return to Town the next day
   for another catch attempt.
3. After Jimothy is caught, a level 28 Primeape appears in Town.
4. Catch or defeat Primeape.
5. Speak to either Pierre or Mayor Lewis to finish the quest.

**Reward:** Jimothy remains in the player's party or personal storage with his
name and grey appearance, and the player receives 5 Great Balls.

### Ghost Club

**Unlock:** Reach two hearts with both Abigail and Sebastian and read the
invitation.

**Preparation:** Bring a healthy level 25 team, healing supplies, and several
Balls. The meeting window is short.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Enter Town before midnight and go to the graveyard.
2. Enter the graveyard meeting area from 11:50 PM to 12:50 AM. The meeting
   starts when you approach; speaking to Abigail or Sebastian also works.
3. Catch or defeat the three level 25 Bad Boos: Gengar, Trevenant, and
   Polteageist. Abigail and Sebastian join as allies.
4. Let the victory scene complete. If the meeting or battle is missed, return
   during the same window on another day.

**Reward:** Haunted Vase recipe. Place and interact with the crafted furniture
to call its associated rematch after the story is complete.

### Late Night Jams

**Unlock:** Reach three hearts, or 750 friendship points, with Sam and read his
letter.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Meet Sam in the Town square from 9:00 PM to 1:00 AM.
2. Catch or defeat the three level 25 Rocking Types: Rillaboom, Toxtricity,
   and Maractus. Sam joins with his partner.
3. Finish the victory scene.

**Reward:** 5 Great Balls, 2 Revives, 250 Sam friendship, and the Mini-Jukebox
recipe. Its furniture can call the associated rematch after completion.

### Ghost Club: Copycats

**Unlock:** Complete **Ghost Club**, reach five hearts with both Abigail and
Sebastian, and read the second invitation.

**Preparation:** This is a large group fight. Bring a strong level 30 or higher
partner, healing supplies, and enough Balls for any Mimikyu that should be
caught.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Meet Abigail or Sebastian in the Town square from 11:00 PM to 1:00 AM.
2. Ten level 30 Mimikyu surround the square. Catching or defeating either
   counts toward the battle.
3. After five Mimikyu have been resolved, an oversized level 35 leader joins
   the fight.
4. Resolve the remaining group and the leader. Abigail and Sebastian join with
   stronger allied partners.

**Reward:** Pikachu Doll recipe. Its furniture can call the Copycats rematch
after completion.

### The Flock Is Loose

**Unlock:** Reach eight hearts, or 2,000 friendship points, with Shane and read
his letter.

The five escaped shiny Psyduck appear at these landmarks:

| Search area | Landmark |
| --- | --- |
| Pelican Town | Joja delivery truck, or the plaza just below the movie theater entrance if it replaced JojaMart |
| Cindersap Forest | Wizard's tower |
| Mountain | Linus's tent |
| Beach | Main dock |
| Farm | Western side of the current farm layout |

The Town Psyduck stays outdoors, so you do not need a movie ticket. If your
Joja-route theater replaced the Community Center, look by the Joja truck as
usual. Building the theater does not reset any Psyduck you already found or
returned.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Visit a landmark and interact with its shiny Psyduck. It begins following
   the quest owner.
2. Speak to Shane to return every currently found Psyduck. Partial turn-ins are
   allowed, so there is no need to find all five in one trip.
3. Revisit any landmark still missing from the count shown in the Journal.
4. Return the fifth Psyduck to Shane.

**Reward:** A player-owned level 20 shiny Psyduck, 3 Pizzas, and 5 Ultra Balls.
The rewarded Psyduck goes to the party if there is room and otherwise goes to
personal storage.

### Something Cheesy

**Unlock:** Enter the Community Center and pick up the green Moldy Cheese that
appears on safe ground near the player.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Pick up the Moldy Cheese. This adds the quest and teaches its recipe.
2. Select the cheese in your toolbar. Hold your Poké Ball throw key (`G` by
   default), aim at safe reachable land, then release. On controller, hold
   Action/Check and release. Dropping it from the inventory does not summon Raticate.
3. Catch or defeat the oversized level 10 Raticate it summons.

**Reward:** Completion of the introductory threat and permanent access to the
Moldy Cheese recipe. Crafted cheese can call later level 10 Raticate rematches.

### Trouble in the Tide

**Unlock:** Own any party or stored Pokémon at level 25 or higher, then read
Willy's letter.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Speak to Willy. He gives the first Smelly Bait and teaches its recipe.
2. Stay on the Beach and select the bait in your toolbar. Hold your Poké Ball
   throw key (`G` by default), aim at safe reachable land, then release. On
   controller, hold Action/Check and release. Dropping bait from the inventory
   does not summon Gyarados.
3. Catch or defeat the forced-shiny level 30 Gyarados.
4. Return to Willy and report the result. Winning the encounter alone does not
   finish the Journal entry.

**Reward:** 5 Ultra Balls and permanent access to the Smelly Bait recipe.
Future bait calls level 45 Gyarados rematches. Willy can provide replacement
bait while the first hunt remains active and no bait is available.

### Clinic Companion

**Unlock:** Reach four hearts with Harvey during Spring, Summer, or Fall and
read the one-time favor letter.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Catch or raise an Audino or Chansey. Either species is valid.
2. Speak to Harvey and choose an eligible individual in the donation view.
3. Confirm the permanent donation.

**Reward:** 10 Revives, Revive recipe, Pokémon Healing Station recipe,
permanently free Harvey party treatment, and a healing resident in the clinic
lobby. This quest is also required for **The Window Watcher**.

### The Window Watcher

**Unlock:** Complete **Clinic Companion**, reach five hearts with Harvey, and
enter Town to see the event outside the clinic.

**Walkthrough:**

1. Finish the event and receive Harvey's Spare Suit.
2. Select the suit in your hotbar and press the normal action button to throw
   it on open reachable land with room for a battle. Hold the button to aim
   farther, then release. Dropping it from the inventory does not summon Mr. Mime.
3. Catch or defeat the oversized level 30 Mr. Mime.

**Reward:** Harvey's clinic companion moves outside the clinic after hours so
its healing service remains available. If the first suit goes missing while
the quest is active, return to Harvey for a replacement.

### Efficient favor preparation

- Keep one non-favorite duplicate of each exact donation species in personal
  storage. Do not assign planned donations to Barn breeding.
- For type requests, any species whose current definition contains the named
  type is valid. **A New Deckhand** accepts any Water type, **A Spark of
  Research** accepts any Electric type, and **Banked Coals** accepts any Fire
  type.
- The frog request is a specific family list rather than a Water-type request.
  A Water species outside the Froakie, Poliwag, Tadbulb, Wooper, and Paldean
  Wooper families is not valid.
- Seasonal requirements control letter delivery, not the deadline. Once a
  valid favor letter has been opened, it can be completed in any season.
- Caught-species gates use distinct registered species, not total captures.
  Evolving and catching new forms can help raise the count.

## Pokémon request board

Complete Willy's **A New Deckhand**, then Robin's **A Place for Requests** to
repair the Pokémon request board. Afterward, Pelipper Town can fill an empty
ordinary Help Wanted day with a Pokémon job.

### Posting schedule

- A Pelipper Town posting appears only when Stardew has not already selected a
  quest of the day. With the original **Help Wanted** mod (0.9.3), it joins that
  mod's expanded board even when Stardew has selected another quest.
- The mod can post up to three requests per season, in the day 2 to 9, day 10
  to 18, and day 19 to 27 portions of the season.
- It does not post on day 1, day 28, a festival day, or the day before a
  festival.
- Year 1 starts with a curated family target in each portion of the season.
- From Year 2 onward, the middle posting is an item request. The first and
  third usually ask for a base species, with a small chance to request a
  previously seen evolution instead.
- Board jobs use Stardew's normal Help Wanted deadline. Unlike one-time mail
  favors, they can expire.

The Year 1 sequence is predictable when each slot is available:

| Season | First posting | Second posting | Third posting |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | Sentret | Geodude | Caterpie |
| Summer | Krabby | Horsea | Poliwag |
| Fall | Gastly | Cubone | Zubat |
| Winter | Seel | Shellder | Voltorb |

### Completing a Pokémon posting

1. Accept the job from the Help Wanted board.
2. Catch, evolve, or retrieve the exact named species. A family member is not
   interchangeable unless that exact evolution is named.
3. Speak to the named requester and choose an eligible Pokémon in the donation
   view.
4. Confirm the permanent donation.

**Reward:** Gold equal to three times the Pokémon's sale value, 2 to 5 Great
Balls, 1 to 3 Potions, a 25 percent chance of 1 Super Potion, and 150 requester
friendship.

### Completing an item posting

Year 2 item postings can request one of these stacks:

| Requester | Target | Gold |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Clint | 3 Poké Balls | 600g |
| Harvey | 2 Potions | 500g |
| Abigail | 1 Repel | 700g |
| Maru | 1 Super Potion | 900g |

Hold the full stack and speak to the requester before the deadline. Item jobs
also grant 150 friendship. Their listed gold replaces the Pokémon sale-value
reward. They still grant the board's random 2 to 5 Great Balls, 1 to 3
Potions, and possible Super Potion.

## Pelican Town Battle League

**Unlock:** Choose a starter or own any Pokémon, enable combat and seasonal
trainers, then read Mayor Lewis's introductory letter.

Visiting Trainers gather at the Town circle every Wednesday and Sunday. In
Stardew's calendar these are days 3, 7, 10, 14, 17, 21, 24, and 28. Speak to
the current trainer with a healthy deployed partner to begin.

Each season has three ordered rungs. A later rung appears only after the prior
rung for that season is defeated. One victory can advance the ladder on a
visiting day. A lost battle can be retried on a later visiting day.

### Finishing the league journal

The Journal tracks two independent requirements:

1. Defeat all three trainer rungs in Spring, Summer, Fall, and Winter.
2. Register 200 distinct caught species.

When both conditions are complete, the quest awards the Champion's Crown. The
Giratina fight after the space-time rivals can also award one. Wear or carry
the Crown and enter the Summit to challenge level 100 Arceus. Wearing the
Crown at a resolved legendary landmark also enables one daily rematch. The
farm's trainer ladders and world encounter history are shared, while the quest
and personal Crown settlement belong to the relevant farmer.

## Four Signs in the Sky

**Unlock:** Meet the Wizard, register 40 caught species, and read the mysterious
letter.

Each sign appears only during its season's final three days. Catching or
defeating it resolves that seasonal sign and raises the next unresolved sign's
level by 5. The first unresolved sign is level 55 and the fourth can reach
level 70.

| Season | Days | Sign | Location |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spring | 26 to 28 | Ho-Oh | Western Cindersap Forest pond |
| Summer | 26 to 28 | Zapdos | Eastern island added to the Beach |
| Fall | 26 to 28 | Moltres | Reachable forge island in the Volcano Caldera |
| Winter | 26 to 28 | Articuno | Mountain lake |

### Walkthrough

1. Read the letter before an eligible window and prepare a strong party,
   status moves, Ultra Balls, and healing supplies.
2. Enter the named map on day 26, 27, or 28. The legendary appears near its
   landmark and remains peaceful until attacked.
3. Catch or defeat the sign. Catching permanently records it and counts toward
   the Journal. Defeating it resolves the sign for progression but prevents
   another ordinary attempt until the same seasonal window next year.
4. Repeat in all four seasons.
5. The Journal itself completes only after Ho-Oh, Zapdos, Moltres, and Articuno
   have all been caught. A defeat advances the sea clue, but the defeated bird
   must still be caught in a future window to close the Journal.
6. After all four signs have been caught or defeated, visit the Beach during
   days 1 to 3 of any season. Lugia appears on the shoreline at level 75.

Articuno, Zapdos, and Moltres each have a one-in-three chance to appear in
their Galarian form, even without incense. Catching either form ends that
bird's ordinary visits. To catch the other form, wear a Champion's Crown and
return to its landmark for a daily rematch; the form is rolled again each time.
The Crown can be earned through the Battle League journal or the space-time
Giratina fight.

The Lugia clue arrives only during an eligible day 1 to 3 window. If the four
signs are resolved on day 4 or later, wait for the next season to begin. A
defeated Lugia is unavailable for the rest of that three-day opening window;
catching it is permanent.

## The Sword and Shield Trial

**Unlock:** Own a Pokémon at level 55 or higher in your party or storage. The
quest enters your log automatically; you do not need to speak to Marlon.

**Objective:** Defeat 300 wild Pokémon from that point onward. Keep at least
one level-55 Pokémon while working on it. Earlier defeats, captures, and VS
Seeker or other trainer victories do not count. The quest log tracks your
progress, and each farmer completes their own trial.

**Reward:** Both ball recipes unlock on the final defeat. Marlon also mails
them the next morning. Craft a Sword Ball with 1 Ultra Ball, 1 Fairy Plate,
and 1 Steel Plate to summon level-70 Zacian. Craft a Shield Ball with 1 Ultra
Ball, 1 Fighting Plate, and 1 Steel Plate to summon level-70 Zamazenta. Throw
either ball onto open reachable land; craft another for a repeat attempt.

## Multiplayer quest rules

- Friendship, one-time letters, personal Journal entries, donations, and most
  quest rewards belong to the farmer who accepted the quest.
- The host owns world actors and wild story encounters. A nearby farmhand can
  help fight, but the quest owner receives the one-time reward.
- Request-board postings and seasonal trainer ladder opportunities belong to
  the farm world.
- Each player should open their own favor letter and use their own valid
  donation. One player's completed personal favor does not grant every
  farmhand another copy of its reward.
- Haley's partner, Marnie's Miltank, Shane's Psyduck, and other story actors are
  shared presentations with owner-specific progress. Let the quest owner make
  the interaction and final turn-in when possible.
- Every player should run the same Pelipper Town build. Version mismatches are
  rejected because they can disagree about quest state and encounter rules.

## Troubleshooting an unfinished quest

### The letter has not arrived

Check every requirement in the favor table, including season, caught-species
count, building, Furnace recipe, Railroad access, and Wizard introduction. Go
to sleep once after meeting the requirement. Also finish or wait seven days
from an earlier open favor if three mail favors are already active.

### A friendship event will not start

Confirm that the friendship requirement belongs to the current farmer, not a
different farmhand. Enter the correct map inside the listed time window. Alex,
Jas, and Jimothy begin through location events; the other friendship stories
first require their invitation letter to be opened.

### A night meeting NPC is missing

Arrive a little before the window and wait on the correct map. Use the exact
meeting area: the graveyard for Ghost Club, the Town square for Late Night
Jams and Copycats, and the Backwoods path for A Safe Place. Ghost Club starts
when you enter the cemetery area even if Abigail or Sebastian's walking
schedule was interrupted. Return on another non-event day if a festival or
another cutscene occupied the map.

### A required Pokémon will not appear

For an ordinary donation, use **Partner Journal > Wild Pokémon** and verify the
target's current habitat rules. For a story target, leave and reenter the map.
The Blue Foal returns after leaving the Forest if it was defeated. Jimothy
returns on the next day after being defeated.

### A Pokémon cannot be donated

Read the block message. Recall active or mounted Pokémon, remove favorite
status, take it out of breeding, unload carried items, heal fainting and
ailments, and keep at least one other owned Pokémon. Type and family favors
also require an exact valid species definition.

### A lure or quest item does nothing

Stand on the required map, aim at safe reachable open land, and make sure the
same encounter is not already active. Smelly Bait works only at the Beach.
Moldy Cheese, Dark Honey, relics, and the Spare Suit need enough space to place
their encounter. The item is not consumed when placement fails.

### The fight ended but the quest remains active

Check the Journal for a report stage. Return to Leah after Zorua, Pierre or
Lewis after Primeape, Willy after Gyarados, Haley with her partner, Jas with
the caught Ponyta, or Shane with found Psyduck. Long journals also remain open
until every counter is satisfied.
